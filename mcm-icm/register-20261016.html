MCM: The Mathematical Contest in Modeling
ICM: The Interdisciplinary Contest in Modeling

The MCM/ICM registration process has been split in to two parts Advisor Registration and Team Registration ($100 per team). Any advisor who wishes to sponsor teams in the 2027 MCM/ICM contest must register via this online system no later than 3pm EST on Thursday, January 28, 2027.

If you are a team advisor and you have not previously registered for this year's MCM/ICM (registration in previous years does not count), enter your email address in the box below and click on the Continue Advisor Registration button.

If you are a team advisor who has already registered for the 2027 MCM/ICM and you would like to register a team or additional teams, go to Advisor Login and enter the email address and password that you used when you registered as an advisor. Once you've logged in to the system you will have an opportunity to register your team(s).

Important: There is a $100 registration fee for each team to participate in the MCM/ICM this year, which you will need to pay with a credit card during the Team Registration process. Please have your credit card ready when you begin the Team Registration process.
