# Meta VR Start Developer Competition: Complete Official Rules

## 1. Sponsor

- Sponsor: **Meta Platforms, Inc.**, 1 Hacker Way, Menlo Park, CA 94025, USA
- 管理方：规则并入 Devpost 服务条款与提交协议（"Administrator" 未在规则页单列；列表卡口径 "Managed by Devpost"）

## 2. IMPORTANT DATES

- **Entry period**: September 24, 2026 at 10:00 AM PT – November 18, 2026 at 12:00 PM PT ("Entry Period")
- **Judging period**: on or about November 18, 2026 – December 9, 2026
- **Winner announcement date**: on or about December 11, 2026

## 3. ELIGIBILITY

- Age 18+；**不面向 Brazil、Quebec 居民**；United States OFAC 制裁地区除外（Crimea region of Ukraine, Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria 等——原文列名照录）
- 须持有 **Meta account with Developer Access**
- **必须已是 Meta VR Start Program 成员**（by the time of submission）
- 员工/家属限制：Meta 与 Devpost 员工及其直系亲属不得参赛（原文照录于规则全文）
- 参与形态：individual（solo）或 Team（2+ individuals, informal grouping, one Team Representative submits）；Organizations 也允许

## 4. How to Enter

- Via devpost.com 在线注册并按提交要求完成 Submission；每人/每组织**限一次 Submission**（"one Entry per individual or Organization"）
- Update 分区（Adapted/Significantly Updated）须为已存在作品的重大更新版本

## 5. Submission Requirements

- **APK**：上传至 Meta VR Developer Dashboard 的 **"Competition" release channel**，权限设为公开并生成 **Invite URL**（或 WebXR 提交链接）
- **Demo video**：**少于 3 分钟**，YouTube/Vimeo 公开链接；须在提交表单提供
- **Submission form**：tagline（≤140 字符）、description（**建议 ≤500 words**）、track（Entertainment/Gaming/Productivity）、division（New / Adapted）、手部交互描述（hand interaction description，optional）、团队信息
- **语言**：Submission materials 必须为 **English**（或附英文字幕）
- **硬性要求（原文）**：
  - "**hands-first end-to-end**"（全程手部交互优先）
  - "policy compliance"（符合 Meta platform 政策）
- **Design principles（原文）**：
  - **Seated-optimized** — "Your experience should be easy to enjoy in a cramped space. If your experience can't pass the **airplane seat test**, it might be time to go back to the drawing board."
  - **Easy in, easy out** — "Design for players on the go. If users can't have a great experience in the time it takes to wait **one bus stop**, consider offering more flexible session lengths or easy ways to save."
  - **Original, not wrapper** — "Thinly veiled ports of existing experiences, like a **YouTube or ChatGPT wrapper** with no added value, don't take full advantage of the immersive possibilities of VR. Think about what your project offers that can't be replicated on a flat screen. **If you removed the headset, would your project still make sense?** If so, you might be missing an opportunity to create something truly unique."

## 6. Tracks and Prizes

> Total ARV of all prizes: **Up to $1,000,000 USD**.（奖金表逐项加总恰为 $1,000,000，见下）

**Track Prizes**（Track: Entertainment / Gaming / Productivity；Division: New Experience / Adapted-Significantly Updated）

| Award | Amount | Winners |
|---|---|---|
| Best New Entertainment Experience | $100,000 in cash | 1 winner |
| Best Adapted Entertainment Experience | $100,000 in cash | 1 winner |
| Best New Gaming Experience | $100,000 in cash | 1 winner |
| Best Adapted Gaming Experience | $100,000 in cash | 1 winner |
| Best New Productivity Experience | $70,000 in cash | 1 winner |
| Best Adapted Productivity Experience | $70,000 in cash | 1 winner |
| Entertainment runner-up（New/Adapted 各一） | $50,000 in cash | 2 winners |
| Gaming runner-up（New/Adapted 各一） | $50,000 in cash | 2 winners |
| Productivity runner-up（New/Adapted 各一） | $35,000 in cash | 2 winners |

**Special Awards**（各 $25,000 in cash，1 winner each）

- **Best Social & Multiplayer** — For the most innovative, engaging, and genuinely social multiplayer experience
- **Best Agentic Interaction** — "For the experience where **AI isn't just a feature—it's a core part of how the world feels and responds**"
- **Best Reason to Come Back** — For the experience with the strongest long-term engagement loop
- **Best First Five Minutes** — For the most engaging and seamless onboarding experience
- **Best Accessibility Forward** — For the most thoughtfully inclusive experience design
- **Boldest Original Concept** — For the most daring, never-seen-before idea

**Judge's Choice Awards**: $20,000 in cash, **2 winners**

- 加总核验：Track 810,000（100k×4 + 70k×2 + 50k×2 + 35k×2）+ Special 150,000（25k×6）+ Judge's Choice 40,000（20k×2）= **$1,000,000**，与 "Up to $1,000,000" 口径自洽。

## 7. Judging

- **Two-stage**：
  - Stage One：评委按 viability（feasibility, originality, overall quality）做 pass/fail 筛选
  - Stage Two：合格 submission 按 4 项**等权 25%** 标准评分：Innovation & Creativity / Experience Design / Technical Implementation / Polish & Presentation
- **Track Award 归属**：每个 submission 参与一个 track + 一个 division；同项目可竞争 special awards
- **AI 相关原文（评审侧）**：
  - "AI tools may also be used to assist in the judging, but final decisions will be made by human judges."
  - Polish & Presentation 准则原文："**Don't lean on AI-generated video to carry the pitch.** Show us real gameplay and be transparent about what stage your project is in."

## 8. LICENSE

- 提交物 IP 归参赛者；Sponsor/Administrator 获**非专属、全球性、免版税**许可用于赛事运营与宣传（展示 submission 材料、姓名/肖像等，规则原文照录）
- 入围/获奖者可能被要求配合后续宣传

## 9. Originality

- Project 必须为 **ORIGINAL**，且**未曾获得 Meta 直接资助**（"must not have received direct funding from Meta"）
- 只可使用 **publicly available and/or Start member tools and resources**
- 违反原创性/资格条款将取消资格

## 10. Conditions（要点）

- 无购买必要（NO PURCHASE NECESSARY）；奖金税负自担；Sponsor 保留取消/修改权利；加州法律管辖（规则尾部 boilerplate，原文照录）
