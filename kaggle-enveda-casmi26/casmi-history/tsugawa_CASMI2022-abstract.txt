We used MS-DIAL to process the data and to determine the adduct form of targeted ions.
A big MS/MS spectral libraries containing both commertially- and publicly available records was imported to MS-DIAL.
Then, some of peaks were assigned by the spectral library information.
For unknown molecules that have not been assigned by the library, we used MS-FINDER.
The result was manually curated.
