Our standard compound ID approach at oloBion uses:

1) SIRIUS for generation of molecular formula candidates
2) an in-house in-silico fragmentation approach that in part utilizes ChemDistiller with organism-specific chemical databases (though not so useful for CASMI!) and custom fragment similarity matching
3) retention time prediction and filtering with Retip 2 utilizing the public PFP natural product dataset on MoNA
4) ranking and filtering of candidates by presence in chemical databases in addition to fragment and retention time similarity scoring

Only matches with reasonable confidence are retained. The same approach was used for the priority and bonus datasets, though it yielded far fewer results for the bonus set.
